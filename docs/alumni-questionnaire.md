# Oxford Medics 96: alumni questionnaire (draft)

Answers will build your profile on oxfordmedics96.com. Only signed-in classmates can see it. Skip any question you like.

## About you

1. Full name, and the name classmates knew you by.
2. Your Oxford college.
3. Confirm you graduated in 1996. If not, which year?

## Then

4. Where did you do your clinical training?
5. A favourite memory from your time at Oxford.
6. Were you involved in Tingewick? If so, how?

## Now

7. Your current job title and specialty.
8. Where you work (organisation and town or country).
9. Briefly, your career path since Oxford (two or three lines).
10. Interests outside medicine.

## Staying in touch

11. LinkedIn profile link (optional).
12. Are you happy for classmates to send you contact requests? (Yes / No)
13. Your preferred contact email (not shown on the site).

## Photos and consent

14. May classmates tag you in photos? Tags appear only after you confirm them. (Yes / No)
15. Would you consider opting in to face matching in future? It would only suggest tags, never publish them. (Yes / No / Not yet)
16. Do you have old photos to share? If so, who is in them and roughly when were they taken?
